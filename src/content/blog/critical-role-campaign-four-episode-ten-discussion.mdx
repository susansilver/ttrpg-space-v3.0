So, hello, it has been awhile. Mostly due to laptop
issues and not being able to update this website. It is
unfortunate but true, I've missed talking about the
past eight episodes. This post will be a wrap up of all episodes
up to ten.

The story that I'm following the most is Thimble. She
is one bad ass rogue. In fact, I am now thinking of playing this
class if I pick up a game again.

We've seen thimble get revenge by killing Casamir. Then
in episode ten she takes out a knight. She is amazing.

I'm also interested in Thimble because she is fae. I'm
wanting to unravel the mystery of why the doors of fairy
are closed. We've learned a few things.

1. Humans hunt and hurt Fae
2. Some humans no longer help Fae because of the preaching of the Candescent Creed
3. Death came for the heart of fairy as Brennan said during the wraith tree battle

Those are the bits that I remember.

In Episode Nine, we meet the Black Rabbits.
Who are fae that can switch their heads between
thorns and blooms. Thimble send them off to tend
the garden of the Gnome they met earlier in their adventure.

Thimble is getting so much focus. But it is clear. There
is something going on with Fairy and Undead that is at the
heart of this story.

I'm probably missing bits. There has been a lot
of lore drops. It is fun discovering things about the world
along with the cast. Seeing what they know or don't know
based on dice roles. Brennan also sometimes reveals things in the
cool down after the episode airs. Which is I've kept my Beacon
suscription.

My favorite thing in game so far are the Hounds of the King. They
are so cool. I love the ones that we have met so far. I'm
hoping we will see them again soon.
